## The second theme

Used from June to October 2026. The site was rebuilt from scratch, with a theme built right into its repository instead of a separate one.

## What defined it

- **A technical-drawing background**: a subtle grid, dotted shapes and blue and red glows.
- **Frosted glass** (glassmorphism) for the navigation bar, cards and panels.
- **A night-blue and red palette**, with Space Grotesk for headings and Inter for text.
- **Work on mobile performance**: blur effects disabled on phones, carousel paused off screen.

This version can still be viewed: the "View this version" tab shows it as it was, rebuilt from its last commit.
