## The first theme

Used from July 2025 to April 2026. The Hugo theme was developed separately, in its own repository ([portfolio.theme](https://github.com/clixmods/portfolio.theme)), and plugged into the site as a Git submodule.

## What defined it

- **An animated night-blue background**, with glowing waves.
- **macOS-style navigation**: a floating dock at the bottom of the screen, and two floating bars at the top (contact, resume, language).
- **A video game touch**: trophies to unlock while exploring the site, with notifications.
- **A gradient title**, "Game Creator + Developer".

This version can still be viewed: the "View this version" tab shows it as it was, rebuilt from its last commit.
