## The current theme

The portfolio looks like a browser: a sidebar of tabs, spaces (Home, Video games, Web & apps, Mods) and the content in a sheet. The idea comes from Arc, Zen Browser and Dia.

## What it brings

- **Tabs and folders**: each project becomes a folder with its overview and links; an experience groups the job description and its projects.
- **Navigation without reloads**: only the page changes, the sidebar keeps its state.
- **Embedded sites**: videos and sites that allow it are shown right in the page, the others open in a new tab.
- **Customization**: each space has its theme (colors, intensity, grain), tabs and spaces can be reordered, the sidebar can be resized.
- **Previous versions**: the older themes stay viewable in the portfolio folder.
